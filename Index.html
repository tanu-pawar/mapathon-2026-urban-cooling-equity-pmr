01 — THE PROBLEM

Cities don't heat evenly.

Urban development changes land cover, vegetation, built-up surfaces and thermal conditions.

At the same time, different populations experience different levels of exposure to heat and different levels of access to cooling resources.

This project investigates where these relationships overlap and where urban cooling interventions may be most important.
